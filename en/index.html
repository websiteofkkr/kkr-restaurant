      Our story

      A table built on tradition

      At KKR Khalid Khan Restaurant, we believe the best food is meant to be shared.

      Rooted in the traditions of Peshawar, KKR brings together authentic flavours, time-honoured recipes, the warmth of live-fire cooking, and the generous hospitality that has always been at the heart of the Peshawari table.

      From a family meal to a gathering with friends, every table at KKR is a place to come together, share a meal, and make memories.
